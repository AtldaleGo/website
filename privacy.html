Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

AtlDale_Go is an independent technology project that develops and tests AI-assisted workflows and integrations.

Information we access

When a user authorizes a third-party integration, AtlDale_Go may access only the information required to perform the workflow the user requested and only within the permissions granted by that user.

How information is used

Authorized information is used to provide, operate, test, and improve the requested workflow. AtlDale_Go does not sell personal information.

Third-party services

Workflows may connect to third-party services through official APIs. Information handled by those services remains subject to their terms and privacy policies.

Google user data

If a workflow uses Google APIs, access to Google user data is limited to the scopes a user explicitly authorizes. Google user data is used only to provide or improve the user-facing functionality requested by that user and is not sold or used for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Data is retained only as needed for the relevant workflow or operational purpose. Users may revoke third-party access through the applicable provider's account controls. Where AtlDale_Go stores workflow-generated data, it may be deleted when it is no longer needed.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to reduce unauthorized access to systems and credentials. Secrets and access tokens are not intentionally published on this website.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the project and its integrations evolve. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
