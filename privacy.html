Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

AtlDale_Go is an independent technology project that develops, operates, and tests AI-assisted workflows, automations, and third-party integrations. This Privacy Policy explains, in general terms, how information may be accessed, used, stored, and protected when users interact with AtlDale_Go services or authorize connected applications.

Information we may access

AtlDale_Go may access information that a user voluntarily provides or explicitly authorizes through a connected service. Depending on the workflow, this may include files, documents, media, metadata, account identifiers, or other information necessary to perform the requested function.

How information is used

Information is used only for legitimate operational purposes associated with the requested workflow, such as processing content, organizing files, generating metadata, performing automation, maintaining records, troubleshooting integrations, or improving the reliability of the requested functionality.

Google user data

When AtlDale_Go uses Google APIs, access to Google user data is limited to the OAuth scopes explicitly authorized by the user. Google user data is used only to provide or improve the user-facing features requested by that user. AtlDale_Go does not sell Google user data, use it for targeted advertising, or transfer it to third parties except as necessary to provide the authorized service, comply with law, or protect against abuse or security threats.

Third-party services

AtlDale_Go workflows may rely on third-party platforms, cloud services, APIs, or automation providers. Those providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. AtlDale_Go does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to support the applicable workflow, operational purpose, legal requirement, or troubleshooting need. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the connected service.

Data deletion and revocation

Users may revoke access to connected third-party accounts through the applicable provider's account or security settings. Where AtlDale_Go stores workflow-generated data under its control, that data may be deleted when it is no longer required for the workflow or upon an appropriate deletion request.

Security

AtlDale_Go uses reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. No system can guarantee absolute security, and users should protect their own account credentials and promptly revoke access they no longer wish to provide.

Children's privacy

AtlDale_Go services are not intentionally directed to children under 13, and the project does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through this website.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the project, technologies, integrations, or legal requirements evolve. Material revisions will be reflected by updating the effective date shown above.
